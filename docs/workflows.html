Workflow 1

Patient journey, counter to report

Along the way

  • Lab numbers restart each day (261004-0007); bill numbers run through the financial year.
  • Payment can be split across cash, UPI and card, or billed to a B2B account.
  • Panels expand into their tests, and a test can't be billed twice.

Tested

  • Every step above through the API, for each role.
  • Discounts need a manager; cancelling refunds and cancels the tubes.
Workflow 2

A tube's life

Tested

  • A tube can't be collected twice or rejected before collection.
  • Rejecting clears any results taken from that tube.
  • Another lab can't find or receive our barcodes.
Workflow 3

Results and approval

Along the way

  • Each result keeps the range it was reported with, so a later catalog change never alters an old report.
  • The patient's previous result shows beside each value.

Tested

  • Low, high and critical flags; formula results can't be typed over.
  • Technicians can't approve; only submitted tests can be approved.
  • An order with approved results can't be cancelled.
Workflow 4

Report delivery

Along the way

  • If some tests are still pending, the PDF says "Partial report"; the link shows new results as they're approved.
  • Every WhatsApp attempt, sent or failed, is kept on the order.

Tested

  • PDF and link work without sign-in; bad or expired tokens are refused.
  • Sending uses the right AiSensy account, number and template (against a stand-in AiSensy).
Workflow 5

B2B billing cycle

Along the way

  • An invoiced order can't be cancelled until its invoice is voided.
  • Account payments count in the day-end collections.

Tested

  • Account rates, the credit limit, invoicing, voiding and part payments.
  • The same orders can't be invoiced twice; overpaying is refused.
Workflow 6

Sign-in, sessions and passwords

Tested

  • Wrong passwords are rate-limited; the reset form never reveals whether an email exists.
  • A replayed old refresh cookie ends the session.
  • Every API route refuses requests without a valid sign-in.
Workflow 7

How permissions are worked out

Tested

  • Each of the six roles against every API route: allowed where the role allows, refused everywhere else.
  • Switching a module off, or taking a permission from the owner, takes effect for signed-in users immediately.
  • No lab can see or change another lab's data.
Workflow 8

Platform admin console

Tested

  • Lab tokens don't work in the console and console tokens don't work in labs.
  • Codes can't be reused; recovery codes work once.
  • A lab always keeps an active owner.
